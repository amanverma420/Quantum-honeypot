⟨ψ| Superposition
A qubit exists as a superposition of |0⟩ and |1⟩ until it is measured. Unlike a classical bit, reading it is not a passive operation — it collapses the state irreversibly. The Hadamard gate creates this superposition for our tripwires.
|ψ⟩ = α|0⟩ + β|1⟩ H|0⟩ = |+⟩ = (|0⟩+|1⟩)/√2
⊗ No-Cloning Theorem
Proven by Wootters & Zurek (1982). It is physically impossible to create a perfect copy of an unknown quantum state. Not a computational limitation — a mathematical consequence of quantum mechanics' linearity.
∄U : U|ψ⟩|0⟩ = |ψ⟩|ψ⟩ ∀|ψ⟩
An attacker cannot duplicate tripwire qubits without disturbing them.
👁 Observer Effect
Measuring a qubit in an incompatible basis irreversibly disturbs its state. If a tripwire is in '+' basis and an attacker measures in '×', the state collapses randomly — creating a measurable fidelity drop.
P(wrong basis) → F_avg ≈ 0.50
Every measurement in the wrong basis leaves a physics-level fingerprint.
≡ State Fidelity
Fidelity measures similarity between two quantum states. F=1.0 means identical; F=0.0 means completely orthogonal. After a wrong-basis measurement, fidelity drops well below the 0.85 alarm threshold.
F(ρ,σ) = |⟨ψ|ϕ⟩|² Alert if: F_avg < threshold (default 0.85)

H — Hadamard Gate
Creates superposition from a basis state. Applied to |0⟩ to prepare tripwire qubits in the '+' basis. Applying H twice returns to the original (H² = I).
H = 1/√2 · [[1, 1], [1, -1]] H|0⟩ = |+⟩ H|1⟩ = |−⟩
X — Pauli-X Gate
Bit-flip gate. Swaps |0⟩ and |1⟩. Combined with H, encodes the '×' diagonal basis variant — giving our two-basis tripwire scheme.
X = [[0, 1], [1, 0]] X|0⟩ = |1⟩ X|1⟩ = |0⟩
M — Measurement
Collapses the qubit to |0⟩ or |1⟩ with respective probabilities |α|² and |β|². Measurement in the wrong basis gives random results and destroys the original state.
P(|0⟩) = |α|² P(|1⟩) = |β|²
F — Fidelity
After decoding, the returned state is compared to the reference |0⟩. Correct basis → F≈1.0. Wrong basis → F≈0.5. Average across N tripwires gives the token score.
F = |⟨0|decoded⟩|² Averaged over all N tripwires
