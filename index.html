⟨ψ| Qubit & Superposition
A qubit exists as superposition until measured. Unlike a classical bit, reading it is not passive — it collapses the state irreversibly.
|ψ⟩ = α|0⟩ + β|1⟩
The Hadamard gate transforms |0⟩ into an equal superposition of |0⟩ and |1⟩ — the basis of our tripwire.
H|0⟩ = |+⟩ = (|0⟩+|1⟩)/√2
⊗ No-Cloning Theorem
Proven by Wootters & Zurek (1982). It is physically impossible to create a perfect copy of an unknown quantum state. This is not computational hardness — it is a mathematical consequence of quantum linearity.
∄U : U|ψ⟩|0⟩ = |ψ⟩|ψ⟩ ∀|ψ⟩
An attacker cannot duplicate tripwire qubits without disturbing them.
👁 Observer Effect
Measuring a qubit in an incompatible basis irreversibly disturbs its state. If the tripwire is in '+' basis and an attacker measures in '×', the state collapses to a random result — producing a measurable fidelity drop.
P(wrong basis) → F ≈ 0.50
Every measurement in the wrong basis leaves a physics-level fingerprint.
≡ State Fidelity
Fidelity measures how similar two quantum states are. F = 1.0 means identical; F = 0.0 means completely orthogonal. After a wrong-basis measurement, fidelity drops below the alarm threshold.
F(ρ,σ) = |⟨ψ|ϕ⟩|²
Alert if: F_avg < threshold (0.85)

H — Hadamard Gate
Creates superposition. Transforms basis states to equal superposition. Used to prepare tripwire qubits in '+' basis.
H = 1/√2 · [[1, 1], [1, -1]]
H|0⟩ = |+⟩    H|1⟩ = |−⟩
Applying H twice returns to the original state (H² = I).
X — Pauli-X Gate (NOT)
Bit-flip gate. Swaps |0⟩ and |1⟩. Used to encode the '×' diagonal basis variant in tripwires.
X = [[0, 1], [1, 0]]
X|0⟩ = |1⟩    X|1⟩ = |0⟩
Combined with H, enables the two-basis tripwire encoding scheme.
M — Measurement
Collapses the qubit to |0⟩ or |1⟩ with probabilities |α|² and |β|². Measurement in the wrong basis gives random results and destroys the original state.
P(0) = |α|²    P(1) = |β|²
This irreversibility is what makes the Observer Effect detectable.
F — Fidelity Measurement
After decoding, we compare the returned state to the original |0⟩ reference using the inner product formula. Correct basis → F ≈ 1.0. Wrong basis → F ≈ 0.5.
F = |⟨0|decoded⟩|²
Average across all N tripwires gives the token fidelity score.
